# How the process works

## Pre-approval
After you send your documents, a pre-approval decision usually takes about 1 business day.

## Documents
Most borrowers provide recent pay stubs, W-2s, tax returns, bank statements and a photo ID. Your loan officer gives you the exact list for your situation.

## Timeline
Most loans close in about 30 to 45 days. Appraisal and title work are part of that time.

## Applying
You can start with a consultation call. Your loan officer then walks you through the application.
