# Loan programs in more detail

## Conventional loans
Some conventional loans are available with as little as 3% down for eligible first-time buyers. Your loan officer confirms whether you qualify.

## FHA loans
FHA loans start at a 3.5% down payment for borrowers with a credit score of 580 or higher.

## VA loans
VA loans are for eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses. Many VA loans do not require a down payment. Eligibility is confirmed with your loan officer.

## Jumbo loans
Jumbo loans are for loan amounts above standard loan limits. Limits change over time, so a loan officer confirms the current amount.

## Minimum credit score
We work with borrowers starting at a credit score of 580.
