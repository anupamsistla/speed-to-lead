# Services

First Lakes Mortgage is a residential mortgage lender serving Minnesota and Wisconsin from offices in Minneapolis, Milwaukee and Madison.

## What we offer
- Purchase loans for buying a home.
- Refinance loans, including rate-and-term refinancing and cash-out refinancing.

## Loan programs
- Conventional loans.
- FHA loans.
- VA loans for eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses.
- Jumbo loans for larger loan amounts. A loan officer confirms the current limits.

## Your loan officer
Every borrower works with one loan officer from start to finish. That person is your single point of contact.

## Free consultation call
A loan officer holds a mortgage consultation call that takes about 30 minutes. It is free and there is no obligation. The loan officer reviews your goals, explains which programs may fit, and lists the documents you would need.
