# Frequently asked questions

## How long is the consultation call?
About 30 minutes, by phone.

## Does the consultation cost anything?
No. It is free and there is no obligation.

## Can I refinance?
Yes. We offer rate-and-term refinancing and cash-out refinancing.

## Which states do you lend in?
Minnesota and Wisconsin.

## What credit score do I need?
We work with borrowers starting at a credit score of 580.

## Can I get pre-approved before I find a home?
Yes. Pre-approval is based on your documents, not on a specific property.

## How do I stop texts?
Reply STOP at any time and we will not message you again.
