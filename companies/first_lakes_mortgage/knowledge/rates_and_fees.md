# Rates, fees and approval

## Interest rates
Interest rates change daily. They depend on your credit score, down payment, loan type and the property. We do not quote rates by text. A loan officer provides a personalized Loan Estimate that shows your actual rate and costs.

## Fees
We do not charge an application fee. Closing costs vary by loan and are itemized on your Loan Estimate.

## Approval
Approval is never guaranteed. Every loan is decided by underwriting after your documents are reviewed.
