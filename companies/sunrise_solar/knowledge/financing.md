# Financing and pricing

## Pricing
Every system is custom designed, so we do not quote a price over text. A price comes with the written proposal after the free in-home consultation.

## Payment options
Sunrise Solar offers three ways to pay, and the energy advisor walks through all three during the consultation:
- Cash purchase.
- Solar loan through our lending partners.
- Lease or power purchase agreement (PPA) on select systems.

Exact interest rates, loan terms and monthly payments depend on the customer's credit and the system size, and are only confirmed in writing by a Sunrise Solar rep.

## Incentives
Tax credits, utility rebates and net metering rules change over time. A rep confirms which incentives apply to a specific home. We do not promise specific savings or incentive amounts over text.
