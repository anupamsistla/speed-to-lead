# Services

Sunrise Solar designs, installs and maintains residential solar systems along the Colorado Front Range.

## What we install
- Rooftop solar panel systems for single-family homes.
- Home battery storage, added with a new system or to an existing one.
- Monitoring app included with every system so you can see production and usage.

## Where we work
Denver, Colorado Springs and Fort Collins metro areas, run from three local offices.

## Free in-home consultation
A Sunrise Solar energy advisor visits the home for about 60 minutes. They look at the roof, the electrical panel and recent electric bills, then prepare a custom proposal. There is no cost or obligation for the consultation.

## Who we can help
We install for homeowners. We cannot install on rented homes because the owner must authorize roof work.

## Typical timeline
After the proposal is signed, permitting and utility approval usually take several weeks, and installation itself is typically one to two days on site.
