# Frequently asked questions

## How long does the consultation take?
About 60 minutes at your home.

## Does the consultation cost anything?
No. It is free and there is no obligation.

## Do I need to be home?
Yes. A homeowner (or someone authorized by the homeowner) should be present so the advisor can review the roof access, electrical panel and your goals.

## What roofs work for solar?
Asphalt shingle, tile and metal roofs are the most common. Flat roofs can work with a mounting system. The advisor checks roof age and condition during the visit.

## What if my roof is old?
The advisor will check it at the consultation. Sometimes a roof should be replaced before solar goes on.

## Can I add a battery?
Yes, batteries can be added to a new system or to an existing solar system.

## Do you work with renters or condo owners?
We only install for homeowners with an eligible roof they control. We cannot install for renters.

## Will solar eliminate my bill?
Results depend on the home, usage and system size. A rep prepares an estimate during the consultation. We do not promise a specific saving.

## How do I stop texts?
Reply STOP at any time and we will not message you again.
