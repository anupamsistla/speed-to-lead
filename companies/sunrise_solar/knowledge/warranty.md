# Warranty

- Workmanship warranty: 10 years on installation work, including roof penetrations.
- Panel warranty: manufacturer's product warranty, typically 25 years, passed through to the homeowner.
- Battery warranty: manufacturer's warranty, terms vary by battery model and are listed in the proposal.
- Monitoring: if production drops unexpectedly, our service team is notified and contacts the homeowner.

Warranty terms are in the signed contract. Sunrise Solar does not guarantee a specific amount of energy production or a specific dollar saving.
